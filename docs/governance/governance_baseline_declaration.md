## 1. Baseline Declaration

The Repository Governance Standard — Sections 1 through 7 and Appendix A, in the form last reviewed — is proposed as **Baseline v1.0.0**, pending formal adoption of version metadata into the Standard's own document header (see GB-002 below). This baseline is the state of the Standard as it stands; this declaration asserts nothing beyond that fact.

## 2. Governance Audit Conclusion

The most recent Governance Audit of the complete Repository Governance Standard concluded: **STABLE WITH MINOR EDITORIAL OBSERVATIONS.** No internal or cross-section contradictions were found. FSM structure, versioning policy, and evidence-management principles were validated as internally consistent. Editorial observations (normative-language convention, terminology glossary, citation convention) were noted as optional improvements that do not affect governance behavior.

## 3. Existing Backlog References

Two backlog items were identified during the audit and are recorded here by reference only:

- **GB-001** — The Repository Decision Matrix (Repository Governance Standard, Section 3) has no row for "Governance artifacts."
- **GB-002** — The Repository Governance Standard's own document header lacks the metadata fields Section 7.9 requires of governance artifacts generally.

This declaration does not define how these items are prioritized, resolved, or tracked going forward — that policy resides in **GEP-001** (Governance Evolution Proposal).

## 4. Closing Statement

This declaration formally records that the Repository Governance Standard, in its current form, is stable and fit for reliance. It introduces no new governance requirement. Any future change to the Standard — including resolution of GB-001 and GB-002, or adoption of any proposal in GEP-001 — occurs only through a future, formally adopted amendment to the Repository Governance Standard, not through this declaration.
